\section{Multi-round convergence (heuristic)}

One round learns a hypothesis from one family $V$. Boundary strings (indecisive
sifts, Lemma~\ref{lem:sound}) are fed back into the representative pool; the next
call to the FNR gate sees a high indecision rate over them and re-clusters to a
family that classifies them decisively.

\begin{assumption}[Coverage]\label{ass:cover}
The prefix sampler $D$ places non-negligible mass on a prefix reaching each
reachable state of $A^\star$; equivalently, every state has representative
rows. (Transient states reachable only by short prefixes are handled by a
prefix-closed ``core''; states reachable by no sampled prefix are provably
unlearnable in this model and are detected by
\texttt{uncoverable\_access\_strings}, which halts the round.)
\end{assumption}

\begin{theorem}[Convergence, informal]\label{thm:main}
Under Assumption~\ref{ass:cover}, and assuming the FNR gate returns, on each round,
a family satisfying Lemma~\ref{lem:denoise} at budget $(\mathrm{fpr},\mathrm{fnr})$,
the round loop resolves at least one previously-boundary state per round until the
tree's leaves are in bijection with the reachable classes of $A^\star$. At that
fixpoint every probe sifts consistently with $t$
(Lemma~\ref{lem:cex} produces no witness), the accuracy estimate certifies
$\Pr_{x\sim D}[A(x)=A^\star(x)]\ge\theta$, and the algorithm terminates. Hence with
probability $\ge 1-\delta$ (union bound over the finitely many
classification, split, and estimation events) the output $A$ is $\theta$-accurate.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
Soundness of the hypothesis is maintained by Lemmas~\ref{lem:sound}
and~\ref{lem:split-sound}: no leaf is ever coarser or finer than the discovered
cuts warrant, w.h.p. Progress is maintained by
Lemmas~\ref{lem:split-complete} and~\ref{lem:cex}: while the tree is strictly
coarser than the reachable Myhill--Nerode partition, a probe eventually exhibits a
detectable disagreement (a state reachable with mass $\rho$ under $D$ is hit by a
probe within $O(1/\rho)$ draws), which splits a leaf; the number of splits is
bounded by $|Q^\star|$. Termination is the accuracy estimate crossing $\theta$,
which by a one-sided binomial bound is a $\le\delta'$ event when the true accuracy
is below $\theta$. The stated probability follows by a union bound over the
$O(|Q^\star|\cdot k)$ denoising events, the $\le N$ split tests, and the estimate.
\end{proof}
